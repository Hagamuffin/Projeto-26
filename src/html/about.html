PARABELLUM
      about

Parabellumis a unofficial, fan-made web application, using the visuals and mechanics from the aclammaid RPG Disco Elysium with the objective of being a tool for creation/managenment of tabbletop RPGS based on the game's features. The following Features were made to simmulate the skills/abilities mechanics as well their respective checks.

Features:

abilities:

  • Each ability (Intellect, Psyche, Physique, Motorics) can have it's level selected within the range from 1 to 6;
  • Skills from the same ability gain a bonus equal to it's level
  • The level of an ability defines the learn cap of a skill from its category

Health and Morale managenment:

  • Health is determined by the sum of the Physique level and Endurence learnt points, whereas Morale is determined by Psyche and Volition
  • Nosaphed and Magnesium can be used to recover Health and Morale points, respectively, following the ratio of 1:1. Its quantities are automaticaly decreased on use and can be manually set by the user.
  • Clicking on an empety bar consumes a healing item, while clicking on a full bar depleats it, changing the respective attribute

Skills managenment and singularity skill:

  • Each of the 24 skills can be leveled up, which is determined by the full diamonds iver the icones
  • A singularity is one skill that receive a +1 bonus on its leaning level, moreover the learning cap of the skills from its ability is increased by one.
  • Dice result is based on the total value from a skill added by the result of 2d6. Which is compared to a result table that contains the range of each succes category. A double 1 is a critical failure and a double 6, a critical success.

Saving the progress:

  • - The character sheet can be saves using the "save" button, exporting its information as a .json file
